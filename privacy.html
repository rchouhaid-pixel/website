Privacy Policy — AGRONAVI Field

Last updated: July 26, 2026

AGRONAVI, an agricultural company located at Commune Sebbah, Douar Oulad Ghanem, P.O. Box 4421, Skhirate, Morocco, provides the AGRONAVI Field Android application (package ID: ma.agronavi.fieldcompanion).

Scope

This policy describes version 1.2.0 of the app. That version works offline, requires no account, displays no advertising, includes no analytics or third-party SDK, and requests no Android permissions.

Data collection and sharing

AGRONAVI does not collect, receive, sell, or share personal data through this version of the app. The app does not transmit user-entered information to AGRONAVI or any third party.

Information stored on the device

The app stores only:

  • field-task checklist selections; and
  • text that the user chooses to save in the field notebook.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on the user's device. AGRONAVI cannot access it remotely. Users should avoid placing unnecessary personal or sensitive information in notebook entries.

The crop guides, calendar, and calculator inputs and results are displayed locally. Calculator inputs and results are not saved by the app.

Retention and deletion

Checklist selections remain until the user resets the checklist, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app. Notebook text remains until the user clears the notebook, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app.

AGRONAVI cannot view, restore, or remotely delete locally stored information.

Device backups

The app disables Android backup for its app data. Device manufacturers or operating-system behavior outside AGRONAVI's control may vary.

Security

Checklist selections and notebook text use Android's private app storage. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and keeping device software updated.

Children

The app is a general agricultural utility and is not designed or directed specifically to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date. Privacy and Google Play disclosures must be reviewed whenever the published app or its included libraries change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page on the official AGRONAVI website or write to the postal address above.
